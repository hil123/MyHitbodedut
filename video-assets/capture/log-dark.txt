Do not worry if at first you do not know quite what to say. Simply open your heart and begin. It can be asking forgiveness, a plain prayer about what you feel, or even a sense of gratitude. Hitbodedut does not have to be orderly or planned in advance. It is a place to release what is in your heart and to turn towards the bond with Hashem.
